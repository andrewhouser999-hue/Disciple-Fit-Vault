# Journal

January->2026
Writing this July 26, 2026

My faith is in full operation. My faith is being tested working solely on the CIC startup. I have a sustaining sense of faith that is life giving, peaceful, confident, grounded that my God will not let me down. That my drive to create CIC is of Him. I can question it, convincing my mind this is just avoidance and seeking to maneuver around repeating the historical cycle as a natural sense of losing interest having to start over yet again with nothing to show for it. But still a quiet settled presence sustains my peace with an inner drive to continue creating CIC.  Seems as though the act of creating CIC would be naturally peaceful.  Outside of prayer, contemplation hearing God's voice, I can rationalize that I am simply enjoying a creative process. However, my heart leaps for joy. Would it leap for joy in coaching sessions running the business? This is where joy must flourish. I have peered into the abyss of long term unemployment with no security in sight more times than I care to admit. I have faced homelessness with no support readily to be had and God provided. I now face dwindling savings, yet I have the King of Kings who takes responsibility for me. I don't fear as I look to Him in my heart. I can fear looking at savings.  

CIC has latent demand, a growing universe but serious competing economic forces. The church is likely an opponent by and large. There may exist opportunities within. CIC will have more institutional people wanting to clone and control this work and not likely to the same pure biblical commitments. 

CIC belong's to the Lord.
