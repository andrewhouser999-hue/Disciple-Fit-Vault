# God's Promises for Life — Personal Reference

A curated (not exhaustive) personal collection of God's promises, ESV, organized by the area of life they speak to. Deliberately excludes promises about identity in Christ (adoption, belovedness, righteousness, etc.) — those are tracked separately. Selection criterion: broad, repeatable relevance to daily life rather than one-time or situational application. Worth checking exact wording against ESV.org before relying on it for memorization or declaration work.

## Presence — He does not leave

- **Deuteronomy 31:6** — "Be strong and courageous... for it is the Lord your God who goes with you. He will not leave you or forsake you."
- **Isaiah 41:10** — "Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God; I will strengthen you, I will help you, I will uphold you with my righteous right hand."
- **Hebrews 13:5** — "I will never leave you nor forsake you."

## Provision — What you actually need

- **Genesis 22:14** — "The Lord will provide" (Jehovah Jireh) — Abraham on Mount Moriah; the naming event behind the whole category.
- **Deuteronomy 8:18** — "remember the Lord your God, for it is he who gives you power to get wealth" — provision through your own labor, still credited to God as source, not self.
- **1 Kings 17:14** — "The jar of flour shall not be spent, and the jug of oil shall not be empty" — Elijah and the widow; provision that outlasts the visible supply.
- **Nehemiah 9:21** — "Forty years you sustained them in the wilderness, and they lacked nothing" — sustained provision across a long, hard season, not just a single rescue.
- **Psalm 23:1** — "The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want."
- **Psalm 23:5** — "You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies... my cup overflows" — provision that continues even while opposition is present.
- **Psalm 34:9** — "those who fear him have no lack!"
- **Psalm 34:10** — "The young lions suffer want and hunger; but those who seek the Lord lack no good thing."
- **Psalm 37:25** — "I have not seen the righteous forsaken or his children begging for bread."
- **Psalm 84:11** — "no good thing does he withhold from those who walk uprightly."
- **Psalm 111:5** — "He provides food for those who fear him; he remembers his covenant forever."
- **Psalm 145:15–16** — "The eyes of all look to you, and you give them their food in due season. You open your hand; you satisfy the desire of every living thing."
- **Proverbs 10:3** — "The Lord does not let the righteous go hungry."
- **Joel 2:25** — "I will restore to you the years that the swarming locust has eaten" — provision as restoration of what was lost, not just forward supply.
- **Matthew 6:31–33** — seek first the kingdom, "and all these things will be added to you."
- **Matthew 7:11** — "how much more will your Father who is in heaven give good things to those who ask him!"
- **Luke 12:24** — "Consider the ravens... they neither sow nor reap... and God feeds them. Of how much more value are you than the birds!"
- **Philippians 4:19** — "my God will supply every need of yours according to his riches in glory in Christ Jesus."
- **2 Corinthians 9:8** — "God is able to make all grace abound to you, so that having all sufficiency in all things at all times, you may abound in every good work."
- **2 Corinthians 9:10** — "He who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will supply and multiply your seed for sowing."

*Deliberately omitted:* Malachi 3:10 (the tithe/"windows of heaven" text) — the most commonly reached-for provision verse, but also the one most easily bent into give-to-get prosperity transactionalism. Left off intentionally rather than silently; add it back with that caution attached if it earns its place.

## Peace — Under real pressure

- **Philippians 4:6–7** — "do not be anxious about anything, but in everything by prayer... let your requests be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and minds."
- **John 14:27** — "Peace I leave with you... let not your hearts be troubled, neither let them be afraid."
- **Isaiah 26:3** — "You keep him in perfect peace whose mind is stayed on you."

## Strength — When you're depleted

- **Isaiah 40:29–31** — "he gives power to the faint... they who wait for the Lord shall renew their strength."
- **2 Corinthians 12:9** — "My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness."

## Guidance — Decisions

- **Proverbs 3:5–6** — "Trust in the Lord with all your heart... and he will make straight your paths."
- **James 1:5** — "If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God, who gives generously... and it will be given him."

## Help in trouble — Not abandoned to it

- **Psalm 34:17–18** — "The Lord is near to the brokenhearted and saves the crushed in spirit."
- **Psalm 46:1** — "God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble."
- **1 Corinthians 10:13** — "God is faithful... he will also provide the way of escape, that you may be able to endure it."

## Rest — Permission to stop striving

- **Matthew 11:28–30** — "Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."

## Prayer — He actually listens

- **Jeremiah 33:3** — "Call to me and I will answer you, and will tell you great and hidden things that you have not known."
- **1 John 5:14–15** — "if we ask anything according to his will he hears us."

## The long view — Why any of this holds together

- **Romans 8:28** — "all things work together for good, for those who are called according to his purpose." Note: to those who love God — a covenant-conditioned promise, not a blanket guarantee.
- **Numbers 23:19** — "God is not man, that he should lie... Has he said, and will he not do it?" — the warrant behind trusting all of the above.
