# Terms

Personal vocabulary reference — words worth knowing, with pronunciation, definition, example usage, and (where applicable) a quote.

## Monachopsis

*mon-uh-KOP-sis*

**Definition:** The subtle feeling of being out of place, as though you don't quite belong wherever you are.

**Example:** Despite the crowd and noise, she felt a quiet monachopsis — a sense of being a misfit in the life she was living.

**Quote:** "You don't always need a reason to feel out of place. Sometimes, it's just part of your story."

## Parochial

*puh-ROH-kee-uhl*

**Definition:** Having a limited outlook; narrow-minded; concerned only with one's own local area, group, interests, or pre-accepted thinking.

**Example:** His parochial Christian views prevented him from seeing an accurate biblical framework or worldview.

## Philautia

*fi-LAW-tee-uh*

**Definition:** The love and care for oneself; a healthy sense of self-worth and self-acceptance.

**Example:** Taking time for your own happiness is not selfish, it's philautia.

**Quote:** "You can't pour from an empty cup, that's not selfish that's philautia."
