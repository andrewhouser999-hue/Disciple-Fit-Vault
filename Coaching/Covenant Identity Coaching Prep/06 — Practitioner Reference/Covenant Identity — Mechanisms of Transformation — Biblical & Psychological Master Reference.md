# Covenant Identity — Mechanisms of Transformation — Biblical & Psychological Master Reference

**What this is:** A single-page, fast-lookup list of every mechanism of transformation CIC actually uses — both the biblical mechanisms (Sections A-B) and the psychological mechanisms adopted alongside them (Section C) — each with its source, its one-line function, and the test or pairing that shows how it relates to Scripture. Built for one specific moment: a rigid, legalistic, or critical challenger (a client, a peer, a pastor) presses "where does Scripture actually say to do that?" or "isn't that just secular psychology dressed up?" — and you need the answer in under thirty seconds, not a document search.

**On Section C specifically:** no single CIC document previously listed the adopted psychological mechanisms in one place with an explicit together/separate framing. That content is split across [[Covenant Identity — Integration Rationale]] (diagnostic-layer contributors, organized by person) and [[Five Intervention Modalities — Definitions & Deep Elaboration]] (intervention-layer, organized by modality) — neither states which mechanisms are used *together with* a specific biblical mechanism versus deployed *on their own*. Section C compresses both into that view. It covers only mechanisms CIC has actually integrated, not the much larger `Coaching Preparation/Practitioner Theory/` survey library (~30 modality files) that was reviewed generally but was not confirmed-adopted into the CIC model specifically.

**What this is not:** It does not perform exegesis and does not replace the source documents. Every row below is a compression of a document that already did the exegetical work — when a challenge goes past the one-line test, hand the conversation to the linked source, don't improvise past what it says.

**How to use it under challenge:** Read the row's **Distinguishing Test** aloud or restate it in your own words. That test *is* the defense — it's the line CIC's own sourcing work already drew between the biblical mechanism and its nearest counterfeit (manifestation, Word of Faith, self-generated certainty, legalistic performance, spiritual bypassing). If the challenger's objection is actually about the counterfeit, the test shows you're not doing that. If their objection survives the test, that's a real open question — take it to the source document, not past it.

## A. Content Mechanisms — what is placed before the person

*What CIC actually declares, reckons, or names as true — the content side of change.*

| Mechanism | Primary Text(s) (ESV) | What It Does | Distinguishing Test (the defense) | Full Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Reckoning** (*logizomai*) | Rom 6:11 — "consider yourselves dead to sin and alive to God in Christ Jesus" | Treat a positionally-true-but-not-yet-fully-experienced reality as true now, and let that reckoning shape behavior. | The *object* must be something God has already declared true in Christ (an indicative — Rom 6:6, 2 Cor 5:17, Gal 2:20), never a self-chosen future outcome the person is trying to will into being through intensity of belief. Certainty about a lie, reinforced daily, is just a more entrenched lie. | [[Covenant Identity — Faith, Certainty & the Reckoning Pattern]] |
| **Renounce → Renew → Receive** | Eph 4:22-24 / Col 3:9-10 — "put off your old self... be renewed... put on the new self" | Gives Identity Installer and Lie Eliminator their three-movement shape: name and release the lie, let the mind be renewed, take up the true identity in its place. | "Put off"/"put on" are aorist — definitive, decisive acts, not techniques to be earned through repeated effort. "Being renewed" is present tense — ongoing, not a one-time transaction. Both are held together, not collapsed into either pure decision or pure passivity. | [[Covenant Identity — Biblical Foundations Index]] §5, Row 1 |
| **Rehearsal / Self-Declaration** | Ps 42:5,11 / 43:5 (refrain); Ps 103:1-2,22 (self-address then itemized rehearsal); Lam 3:21-24 ("says my soul") | Grounds the practice of spoken, repeated, first-person declaration — the literal form Lie Eliminator and Identity Installer use. | Modeled from *inside* unresolved distress, not from a place of already-arrived stability — the psalmist declares while still grieving, not after. Distinct from Deut 6/8's rehearsal pattern, which is teacher-to-child instruction, not self-to-self declaration — that text supports the *adjacent* claim (repetition internalizes truth), not this specific practice; the two are not folded together. | [[Covenant Identity — The Biblical Pattern of Self-Declaration — Practitioner Exegetical Reference]] |
| **Witnessing Repentance & Confession** | Ps 139:23-24 (prayer — "search me... see if there be any grievous way"); Luke 15:17-19 (recognition before confession — "he came to himself," *then* "I will say"); 1 John 1:9 (act-level confession) | Two levels: quick, bounded confession of a specific act (1 John 1:9) — and a deeper, Spirit-invited *witnessing* of what is actually being trusted, which must be seen before it can be turned from. | Godly grief turns *toward* God and invites more exposure (Ps 139's own posture); worldly grief collapses *into* the self and hides (Gen 3:10; 2 Cor 7:10 names the variable). Judas's anguish ended in self-destruction, Peter's in restoration — same intensity of grief, opposite direction. Witnessing must be Spirit-invited (a prayer *to* God), never coach- or self-manufactured through shame pressure — the moment a coach becomes the one exposing the client's sin rather than creating conditions for the Spirit to do so, this has drifted from formation into correction. | [[Covenant Identity — Indwelling Sin and Witnessing Repentance — Mechanism]] |

## B. Participatory Mechanisms — the conditions the person creates

*What Scripture commands the human side to do, while naming God/the Spirit as the actual agent of the change produced. None of these are self-effort mechanisms; each is a condition under which transformation the person cannot manufacture is received.*

| Mechanism | Primary Text(s) (ESV) | What It Does | Distinguishing Test (the defense) | Full Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Beholding** — the central mechanism text | 2 Cor 3:16-18 — "beholding the glory of the Lord, are being transformed... from the Lord who is the Spirit" | The explicit, sequential mechanism: turning → unveiling → beholding → transformation. Governs CIC's whole attentional layer. | The verb is passive — *are being transformed* — the human act (turning, beholding) creates the condition; transformation is received, not achieved. CIC's own Interpretive Commitment (Practice Definition Dimension 2) lands this as **Word-mediated and disciplined** (Willard's VIM) — not an open, contentless contemplative gaze, and not itself produced by diagnostic precision or technique. | [[Covenant Identity — Conditions for the Spirit's Sanctifying Work]] §I.B |
| **Walking / Keeping in Step** | Gal 5:16, 25 — "walk by the Spirit... let us keep in step with the Spirit" | Names a sustained orientation, not an episodic effort — the condition under which the Spirit's fruit grows. | The fruit (καρπός, organic) is not achieved by effort; it grows in someone who is walking. This blocks the misreading that spiritual fruit is a performance target to hit through willpower. | [[Covenant Identity — Conditions for the Spirit's Sanctifying Work]] §I.A |
| **Abiding** | John 15:4-10 — "Abide in me, and I in you... apart from me you can do nothing" | Relational, continuous connection as the source of fruit, with identifiable content (his words abiding, keeping his commandments). | Not passive mysticism — abiding has stated content and is sustained over time, not a feeling to chase. | [[Covenant Identity — Conditions for the Spirit's Sanctifying Work]] §I.A |
| **Renewing the Mind** | Rom 12:1-2 — "do not be conformed... be transformed by the renewal of your mind" | Names two competing formative pressures (the world's conforming mold vs. the Spirit's renewing work) and locates the human's part as *refusing* conformity, not manufacturing renewal. | Both verbs are present passive — renewal happens *to* the mind, not *by* it. The human act is presenting the body as worship and refusing the world's mold (v.1); a client without deliberate counter-attentional practice is not neutral, they are being actively conformed to something. | [[Covenant Identity — Conditions for the Spirit's Sanctifying Work]] §I.B |
| **Asking / Persistent Petition** | Luke 11:9-13 ("how much more will the Father give the Holy Spirit to those who ask"); Gal 3:2, 5 (received "by hearing with faith," not works of the law) | Names asking — specifically persistent, ongoing asking — as a real condition for receiving the Spirit's work, not a formality before the "real" mechanism starts. | Set inside the persistent-friend parable — the condition is *ongoing* asking (present tense), not a one-time petition treated as a technique that guarantees an outcome. | [[Covenant Identity — Conditions for the Spirit's Sanctifying Work]] §I.C |
| **Lament-to-Trust** | ~40% of the Psalms; Ps 22 (opens "why have you forsaken me," closes in praise); Job; Lamentations | Honest complaint before God as the path *into* trust, not around it — naming pain is itself covenant engagement, not a breach of faith. | Distinct from spiritual bypassing (Wilber) — using spiritual language to avoid emotional reality, which produces people who appear stable but are frozen, not transformed. Brueggemann's orientation → disorientation → reorientation: you cannot skip disorientation to reach reorientation; attempting to jump straight to praise produces "cheap grace," not faith tested by honest engagement with darkness. | [[Five Intervention Modalities — Definitions & Deep Elaboration]] §4 |

## What This List Deliberately Leaves Out

- **The Ten-Layer Identity Architecture's content** (already-true realities like Adopted Child, New Creation, Standing Secure) — those are the *declared truths themselves*, not the mechanism by which they're applied. See [[Covenant Identity — Biblical Foundations Index]] §3.
- **The diagnostic layer** (affections, God-exclusion, character wound typing) — those identify *what* needs to change, not the mechanism of change itself. See [[Covenant Identity — Biblical Foundations Index]] §2.
- **The broader `Coaching Preparation/Practitioner Theory/` survey library** (~30 modality files — SDT, ACT, MBCT, CFT, Reiss Motivation, and more) — reviewed generally during the coaching practice's development but not confirmed-adopted into the CIC model specifically. Section C above lists only what CIC has actually integrated.

## Open Gap — Flagged, Not Filled

**Filling of the Spirit (Eph 5:18 — "be filled with the Spirit," present imperative, continuous).** Surfaced by cross-checking the DTS comparison list, where it's named as the central mechanism and "surprisingly absent from most change lists." No CIC document currently names this as a distinct, sourced mechanism in its own right — the nearest CIC content (Beholding, Walking, Abiding) covers adjacent ground but none anchors specifically to Eph 5:18. Not added here because no CIC document has done the exegetical work yet; flagged so it isn't silently missing. If a challenger raises "what about being filled with the Spirit specifically," that is currently a real gap, not a covered one — say so rather than retrofitting an answer.
