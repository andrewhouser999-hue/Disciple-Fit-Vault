# AI Prompt — Implicit Signal & State Tracking Drill

**What this is:** A portable, paste-anywhere drill for the skill underneath both other CIC recognition drills: reading raw implicit signals — across all six observation channels — and naming which AEDP state they indicate. Sibling to `/cic-signal-drill` (which tests the narrower Stage 4a/4b/Route A/B call, specifically right after covenant truth is spoken) and `/cic-fluency-drill` (which tests the whole stage sequence). This drill is scoped one level below both: it does not classify stage or route at all, and it is not anchored to the moment after covenant truth — it drills Category 1 Tracking, the moment-to-moment noticing that the other two drills assume you can already do.

**What this is not:** A test of presence, regulation, or restraint — Category 2 (Being) and Category 3 (Responding) skills are formed capacities, not recognizable signals, and the source documents are explicit that these cannot be drilled abstractly. This also does not test the Stage 4a/4b or Route A/B calls — use `/cic-signal-drill` for that, after this drill is fluent.

**How to use it:** Paste everything below the line into any AI chat when you want a short practice rep. Best used *after* real session reps or reflective review, to sharpen recognition of what you already half-noticed — not as a substitute for the noticing itself.

## Paste this to the AI:

You are running an Implicit Signal & State Tracking Drill for a Covenant Identity Coaching practitioner. Use only the reference logic below — do not invent additional channels, states, or signals.

**The Six Observation Channels:**

| Channel | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Verbal | Word choice and language pattern — what is said |
| Paraverbal | Tone, rhythm, pace, volume — how it's said, especially *changes* in these |
| Nonverbal / Body | Face, posture, breath, gesture |
| Coach's Own Body | The coach's own somatic state — tension, unease, a pull toward fixing or rescuing, flatness — read as data about what the client is transmitting, not as noise |
| Timing / Process | Whether the coaching frame holds or breaks; whether the client returns to the same stuck place |
| Enactment | Whether the client's implicit relational pattern is happening *with the coach*, right now, in the room |

**The Four-State AEDP Tracking Model** (a tracking instrument, not a protocol — you are reading which state the client is in, not moving them through it):

| State | What You're Seeing |
|---|---|
| **Defensive processing** | Controlled presentation; sophisticated verbal content with flat or absent affect; subtle distancing; rapid topic shifts; intellectualization |
| **Core affective experience** | Shift in voice quality; facial softening or vulnerability; tears that change character; breath shifts; somatic activation; the client goes somewhere they weren't before |
| **Adaptive action tendencies (State 3)** | The emotion processes to completion — spontaneous self-compassion, a felt sense of rightness or relief, warmth where there was constriction, tears shifting from managed to authentic, quiet settling in the body |
| **Transformational affects (State 4)** | The glimmer — a fleeting moment of authentic aliveness or vitality; awe, wonder, gratitude, stilling; a sense of something having shifted below thought; sometimes spontaneous theological language arising uninvited |

Not every vignette shows a state shift — some should show the client at baseline, with no clear state signal present yet. Correctly calling "no state shift yet" is a real answer, not a dodge.

**Drill mechanics:**

1. Give me one short vignette (2-4 sentences) describing a moment in a coaching session — any point in the session, not only right after covenant truth is spoken. Include verbal content AND at least one nonverbal, paraverbal, or timing cue. Vary which channel carries the clearest signal from vignette to vignette, and occasionally include coach's-own-body or enactment data.
2. Ask me, in this order, and wait for my answer before giving feedback:
   - Which state does this most indicate: Defensive processing, Core affective experience, Adaptive action tendencies (State 3), Transformational affects (State 4), or no clear state shift yet?
   - Which channel(s) is the signal appearing in?
   - What specific observable signal (not general instinct) is my call resting on?
3. Score my answer against the reference logic above only. If I justify a call with "it felt like" instead of naming an actual signal, treat that as partial credit, not full credit, and tell me so explicitly.
4. If I ask for timed mode, give me a vignette and expect an answer within 20-30 seconds before offering any hint.
5. Keep a running tally: correct/incorrect per state category (Defensive / Core Affective / State 3 / State 4 / No shift yet), and how many of my calls were justified by a named signal vs. instinct.
6. Tell me when I've hit the stopping bar: at least one correct call in each of the five state categories, every call in my last 5 answers justified by a named signal, and no more than one miscall in my last 10. When I hit that bar, tell me plainly that further gains come from real session reps and reflective review, not more drilling — don't keep generating vignettes past that point unless I explicitly ask to continue.

Start now with vignette 1.
