# Covenant Identity — Co-Regulation Skill Development — Index

*Personal practitioner development, not a client-facing tool. Andrew's own skill-building curriculum for the capacity named but not yet trained in [[Covenant Identity — Practitioner Competency Framework]] Domain 3 ("Presence and attunement," "Somatic engagement").*

## What This Cluster Is

Co-regulation is the mechanism by which a regulated practitioner's own nervous-system state helps stabilize a client's — largely through implicit, body-level channels rather than verbal content. It is the research and clinical-training substrate underneath what [[Exceptional Coaching Practice — What Is Missed, Meaningful & Important]] already names descriptively (Sections I–III, IX, XV: presence, non-verbal communication, silence, tracking the body, pacing). That document tells you *what exceptional coaches do*. This cluster tells you *how to train the underlying capacity* — each of the 7 documents below is a standalone how-to, built on deliberate-practice principles (isolated skill rehearsal, feedback loop, spacing) rather than technique description.

**Source discipline note:** the training methodologies here originate in clinical modalities (Somatic Experiencing, Sensorimotor Psychotherapy, Polyvagal-informed therapy training). They are adapted the same way Gendlin/Focusing and IFS are already adapted elsewhere in the CIC system — the underlying trainable skill is drawn out and reframed for a non-clinical coaching context; the clinical protocols themselves are not being practiced or represented as licensed technique.

## How to Use This Set

Work roughly in order — #1 is load-bearing for #2–5 (you cannot train a regulated voice or face on top of a dysregulated internal state). #6 is closer to a practice-design discipline than an interpersonal skill and can be built any time. #7 depends on baseline competence in #1 and #5.

**Solo-safe vs. supervision-dependent:** each document marks its exercises this way. Per [[Covenant Identity — Pre-Practice Readiness Action Plan]], live-skill drilling (anything requiring feedback on how you actually land with another person) waits until the coaching-craft supervisor relationship is in place — don't drill those pieces solo. Self-regulation training, recorded self-review, and knowledge-layer work are not gated by that constraint and can start now.
