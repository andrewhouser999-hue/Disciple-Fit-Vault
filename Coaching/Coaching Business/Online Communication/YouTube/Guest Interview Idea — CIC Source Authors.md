# Guest Interview Idea — CIC Source Authors

**Status:** Idea captured 2026-10-19. Not scheduled, not decided, no outreach made.

**The idea (Andrew, 2026-10-19):** Write up a list of the authors, coaches, and psychologists whose work is primary to CIC's foundational source material, as potential interview guests for the YouTube channel. The "following" in the idea means the audience that channel builds. Guests who are named sources of the model would bring that following into contact with CIC's foundations.

**What this file is:** A first-pass candidate list drawn only from sources already in the vault. No new research was done. It is a starting inventory to react to, not a final guest list.

---

## Read This Before Using the List

Five judgments, flagged as design judgment (not sourced fact):

1. **Living/deceased status below comes from general knowledge, not a live check.** Verify every name before any outreach or on-camera reference.
2. **Sequencing matters more than the list.** The channel has no published video yet ([[_YouTube Channel — Status]]). A cold request to an author from a channel with nothing on it gets a low yes-rate. Recommended gate: contact no one until Video 01 is published and a few deep-bench videos exist, so a guest can see what they would be appearing next to.
3. **An interview is borrowed authority, not peer proof.** The Status file says the channel's strategy is to compete on precision and peer proof. A famous guest does not replace either. Decide what job the interview does before committing to the format (see Open Decisions).
4. **A guest is not bound by the Marketing Charter.** The Charter's guardrails (no institutional critique, no clinical language, no Word-of-Faith adjacency) apply to Andrew's own scripts. A guest can say anything on camera. Several names below have an explicit guest-fit flag for this reason.
5. **Interviewing the people whose books ground the model invites scrutiny of the practice.** The [[Covenant Identity Coaching — Quick Launch Plan]] already flags that zero live sessions have been run and no supervisor is confirmed. A guest who knows the field will notice both.

## Reachability — Design Judgment, Not Researched

- **Likely most open to a small-channel conversation:** researchers and mid-profile practitioners (Hall, Kirkpatrick, de Haan, Ecker, Barton). Academics and working practitioners more often say yes to a respectful, specific request.
- **Long shots:** the highest-profile names (N.T. Wright, Siegel, Brueggemann, Kegan). A first request would need the channel to have real content on it.

This is a hypothesis about outreach behavior, not something the vault or any research supports. Treat it as a guess to test, not a plan.

## Open Decisions (Andrew)

1. **What job does the interview do?** Three candidates: borrowed credibility for the Wilderness Christian audience; Stage 3 (Curiosity) mechanism-explanation content; or a source of clips for deep-bench fill. The job decides which guests matter.
2. **Format:** solo-hosted conversation, or a shorter structured Q&A built around one narrow question per guest? A narrow question lowers the ask and lowers the chance a guest drifts off-guardrail.
3. **Pre-brief:** whether guests get a written one-paragraph frame (audience, what the channel will not do, topic boundaries) before recording. Several guest-fit flags above could be handled this way.
4. **Gate:** confirm or change the recommended gate (Video 01 published plus a few bench videos) before any outreach.
5. **Scope of "coaches":** the vault's coaching-methodology names (Whitmore/GROW, CTI/Co-Active, Miller & Rollnick, Cooperrider, de Shazer) are in the Source Library's Methodology Index but not given a seat here. Add them if "coaches" in the original idea meant them.
