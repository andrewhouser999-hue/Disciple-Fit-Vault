# Video Content — Drafts & Working Notes

*What this is: a scratch workspace for scripting/drafting video content — not the live plan. Write freely here; nothing is "official" until it's copied into a video's own Adaptation Plan file (like [[Video 01 — Adaptation Plan]]). Shorts aren't scripted separately — they get cut from whatever long-form video is drafted here, per [[Wilderness Christian]]'s channel notes, so there's no separate Shorts drafting section below; just flag which lines in a script would cut well as a Short when you notice one.*

## Checklist to run on every video before it's considered ready

Pulled from the Marketing Charter — applies to video exactly like it applies to the website:

- [ ] Leads with felt cost, not root — no diagnostic/theological-root language before recognition has landed (Buyer's Journey Stage 2)
- [ ] No urgency or scarcity language
- [ ] No generic Christian growth language ("become who God made you to be")
- [ ] Precision of language naming the interior experience — the strongest available trust signal; generic phrasing doesn't do this
- [ ] Guardrails held: no Word-of-Faith adjacency, no institutional critique, no clinical language
- [ ] Voice: calm, precise, unhurried — cut anything that could read as a fitness-influencer Instagram caption
- [ ] CTA matches the current channel sequencing (see Video 01 below for the live open question on this)

## Video 01 — adapted from *The Mind You've Never Renewed*

**Status:** Adaptation Plan built ([[Video 01 — Adaptation Plan]]); title, cold open, and structure set. **CTA still undecided** — three options on the table (comment-to-connect / subscribe-only / point to the website). Revisit the website option once the homepage is actually published, not just drafted.

**Flag, 2026-09-17:** the current primary title ("The Part of Your Mind Sermons Were Never Built to Reach") risks the Marketing Charter's institutional-critique guardrail — it frames sermons as insufficient before the video's own companion framing has a chance to land. Recommend swapping to one of the sermon-free alternates below.

*Title alternates to test:*
- Why You Can Believe It and Still Not Feel It
- Why Knowing God Loves You Isn't the Same as Feeling It
- The Gap Between What You Believe and What You Feel
- (existing runners-up, still solid, neither mentions sermons: "Why You Still React the Same Way After Years of Faith" / "Why Knowing the Truth Isn't Changing You")

*Cold-open alternates (current: "You can know God loves you...and it's not what you think"):*
- Door 6 angle: "You've prayed this away more times than you can count. It's still there. Today I want to show you why — and it's not because your faith is thin."
- Door 7 angle: "You're not walking away from God. You never did. But somewhere along the way it went flat, and no one's ever really explained why."
- Tightened original: "You can know God loves you, defend it, even teach it to your kids — and still not feel it. I want to show you why. It's not what you think."

*CTA drafts (all three options, so they're ready to compare side by side):*
- **Comment-to-connect version:** "If this named something in you — if you found yourself thinking, that's the thing I could never quite put words to — I'd like to hear about it. Comment 'layer' below and I'll respond myself. Not a funnel. I read these."
- **Subscribe-only version:** "If this named something in you, that's enough for today — I don't need anything else from you right now. If you want more of this kind of thinking, subscribe. There's more ahead."
- **Point-to-website version:** "If this named something in you, I've put together a place to go deeper — a short way to see whether this is worth pursuing further. No pressure, no pitch. Link below." (only viable once the site is actually published, not just drafted)

*Lines that would cut well as a Short:*
- "This is why you can know that God loves you and still not feel loved by Him."
- "This is why you can affirm that you are a new creation and still respond to failure like someone who is not."
- "A slave needs to be set free — not just told they are free."
- "Not gritting your teeth to stay in the Word — but actual desire for it."

*Reference — approved umbrella/end-card line (not for title/hero use):* "Renew the Mind Never Touched by the Word" — Marketing Charter, Brand Type Rule, resolved 2026-09-16.
