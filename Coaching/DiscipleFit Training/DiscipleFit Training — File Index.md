# DiscipleFit Training — File Index

DiscipleFit Training holds delivery-layer lesson material (video scripts, teaching content built for distribution) as distinct from Covenant Identity Coaching (`Coaching/Covenant Identity Coaching Prep/`), which is the coaching methodology itself. CIC may draw on this folder as a resource alongside its own established content; the same foundational content here may also inform Formation & Transition, with structural/medium adaptation only. See [[feedback_practice_separation]] for the full practice-architecture decision (2026-09-16).

## Files

- **Sin as Missing the Mark — Hamartia Video Script.md** — 3–4 min video script on the applied meaning of ἁμαρτία (hamartia) for believers; foundational trust-building content, reusable (with adaptation) for CIC and Formation & Transition. Added 2026-09-16.
- **God's Will for Your Life — Proverbs 3 Sermon Sketch.md** — full-length sermon on Prov 3:5-6 and knowing/walking in God's will; companion piece to the Hamartia script (same voice, longer sermon form). Working sketch, not yet delivery-ready. Added 2026-09-17.
